## 🌟 About

**VendorOS AI** is a smart retail management platform that helps small businesses manage **inventory, sales, customers, suppliers, and business insights** in one place.

> **Manage better. Analyze smarter. Grow faster.**

---

## ✨ Features

- 📦 **Inventory** — Products, stock, SKU & expiry tracking
- 💰 **Sales & Billing** — Transactions, revenue & automatic stock updates
- 👥 **Customers** — Customer records & purchase history
- 🚚 **Suppliers** — Supplier and contact management
- 🤖 **AI Insights** — Sales trends, product insights & business intelligence
- 📊 **Dashboard** — Revenue, inventory health & business alerts
